May 16–August 5, 2026

Come watch the Portland Pickles and get a pickle tattoo while you’re there!

There were different themes throughout the season and we usually had new flash for each theme. Of course, everyone could also look through our massive catalogue of past pickle themes and choose a pickle that spoke to them!

These game-day tattoos were flash only, with no customs that day, and arms and legs only please.

If you missed us at the games, you can still request one of my past pickle designs through my [booking form](/booking/).
