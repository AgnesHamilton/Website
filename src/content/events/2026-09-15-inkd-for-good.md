Get tattooed and do some GOOD! I’m excited to be participating in Ink’d for Good again this year!

Ink’d for Good is an online fundraiser where tattoo artists donate their time, you purchase a tattoo session, and 100% of the proceeds support two amazing local nonprofits: PDX Diaper Bank and Baby Blues Connection.

Sales are open September 15–30, while sessions last. These tend to go quickly, so don’t wait if you want one!

[View my available Ink’d for Good tattoo sessions.](https://www.inkdforgood.com/product-category/tattoo-artists/agnes-hamilton-anatomy-tattoo/)

Thank you for helping support Portland families—and for getting a fun new tattoo while you’re at it! ❤️
