Come eat, drink, shop and get tattooed at the Portland Night Market on October 2 and 3, from 4–11pm!

This is a magical, free, all-ages community event—but you can buy a Fast Pass if you’d like to skip the wait in line. Stop by the Anatomy Tattoo booth to say hi and get tattooed! We will have tons of available flash designs for you, with previews coming soon.

The theme is still to be announced, so check back for more details!

A huge thank you to everyone who has come by our booth at previous Night Markets. It’s always so lovely to meet you all, and I can’t wait to do it again!
