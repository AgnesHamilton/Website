Calling all lovers of the strange and unusual! The Oddities & Curiosities Expo is coming to Portland November 7 and 8 at the Oregon Convention Center.

The expo brings together artists, small businesses and vendors with all kinds of weird artwork, antiques, curiosities, creepy clothing, odd jewelry and more.

Anatomy Tattoo is hoping to join the fun with a tattoo booth, but the details are not confirmed yet. Save the date and check back for updates about artists, flash and tattoo availability.
