DOG FRIENDS! Join me at Wonderland Tattoo on Sunday, September 27 from 11am–5pm for a tattoo flash fundraiser benefiting Stumptown Strays! 🐾

100% of the proceeds will go to Stumptown Strays so they can keep helping sweet pups get the care they need and find their forever homes. Our goal is to raise $10,000!

There will be special flash tattoos and a raffle, with more details coming soon. Come get tattooed, enter the raffle and help us raise some money for a really wonderful organization!

[Learn more about Stumptown Strays.](https://www.stumptownstrays.org/)

[See the fundraiser announcement on Instagram.](https://www.instagram.com/p/DcjrY5nGSef/)

Designs coming soon. I hope to see you there! 🐶
